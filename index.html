KNOW THE SIGNS

How to Spot a Phishing Attempt

Learn the warning signs that can help you avoid online scams.

📨

Suspicious Sender

Check the complete email address. Scammers may use addresses that imitate trusted organizations.

CHECK THE SOURCE
⏰

Urgent Messages

Messages threatening account suspension or demanding immediate action may be phishing attempts.

PAUSE & THINK
🔗

Unexpected Links

Check the displayed domain carefully. When in doubt, visit the official website directly.

VERIFY THE URL
🔐

Requests for Secrets

Never share your password, one-time password, or recovery code through an unsolicited message.

PROTECT YOUR DATA

Can You Spot the Red Flags?

Review these fictional examples. Do not click unknown links in real messages.

✉️
Example 01 — Account Warning

From: security-alert@example.invalid

SUSPICIOUS

Your account requires immediate attention!

We noticed unusual activity. Your account will be restricted unless you verify your details immediately.

[Unverified link hidden for safety]

⚠ Red flags: Urgent language, an unverified sender, and a request to verify account details.

🎁
Example 02 — Unexpected Prize

From: rewards@example.invalid

SUSPICIOUS

Congratulations! You have won a prize!

You were selected for a reward. Provide personal information and pay a processing fee to claim it.

[No real claim link provided]

⚠ Red flags: An unexpected reward, a request for personal information, and an upfront fee.

These are fictional training examples. The reserved example.invalid domain is used to avoid directing visitors to real phishing sites.
